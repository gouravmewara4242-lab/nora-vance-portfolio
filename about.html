About Me · India

Hi, I’m Gourav—an editor obsessed with pacing, light, and human truth.

I fell in love with video storytelling when I realized that cutting two seconds of silence before a punchy hook could change the entire emotional retention of an audience.

Over the past 1.5 years, I’ve worked remotely from India with Indian D2C brands, YouTube educators, architects, and independent creators worldwide. My specialty sits right at the intersection of high-retention 9:16 vertical reels, filmic color grading, and clear narrative scriptwriting—where every cut feels calm, premium, and intentional.
